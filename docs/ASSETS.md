# Included visual assets

The three nature wallpapers in `launcher/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/` were generated
specifically for this open-source project on October 25, 2026. They contain no imported logos,
text, people, or recognizable landmarks.

The generation directions were:

- A serene misty mountain valley at blue hour, composed as a quiet 16:9 television background.
- A still alpine lake surrounded by evergreen forest and distant reflected mountains, composed
  as a quiet 16:9 television background.
- A rugged coastline with gentle waves and sea mist, composed as a quiet 16:9 television
  background.

All prompts requested an understated photographic style and excluded text, logos, watermarks,
buildings, and recognizable landmarks.

Application icons shown at runtime are loaded from the applications installed on the user's Fire
TV. They are not bundled with this repository.
